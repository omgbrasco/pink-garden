How I work

I live only on your phone. I don't have internet access, and nothing you say to me ever leaves this app.

When you added me to your home screen, you installed a "web app" - a tiny website that acts like a real app, works without wifi, and remembers things using your phone's own storage instead of a company's server.

Nothing here is watched, saved online, or used to train anything. Just you, me, and this garden.
